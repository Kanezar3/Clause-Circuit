EUROPEAN COMMISSION - DIRECTORATE-GENERAL FOR COMPETITION
Case M.11402 - Helios Industrial Holdings / NordGrid Photonics GmbH
Public Notice of Phase I Decision (10 November 2026)

Following notification under Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004, the Commission examined the proposed acquisition of sole control over NordGrid Photonics GmbH by Helios Industrial Holdings. After reviewing horizontal overlaps and vertical supply relationships across the European Economic Area, the Commission concluded that the notified operation does not raise serious doubts as to its compatibility with the internal market and therefore approved the transaction unconditionally at Phase I under Article 6(1)(b) without divestiture or behavioral commitments.
