COMMITTEE ON FOREIGN INVESTMENT IN THE UNITED STATES (CFIUS)
Docket No. CFIUS-2026-08914 - Notice of Extended Review Status (12 November 2026)

In connection with the proposed acquisition of NordGrid Photonics GmbH by Helios Industrial Holdings, the Committee has completed the initial 45-day review and entered an extended national-security investigation concerning photonics export controls and domestic optical supply assurance. As of the date of this notice, CFIUS has not granted clearance for the transaction; proposed mitigation covenants remain under negotiation and no clearance determination has been issued to authorize closing.
