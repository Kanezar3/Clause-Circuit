COMPETITION AND MARKETS AUTHORITY (UNITED KINGDOM)
Case ME/7118/26 - Phase 1 Merger Inquiry Summary (14 November 2026)

On the information currently available to it under the Enterprise Act 2002, the Competition and Markets Authority has decided not to refer the anticipated acquisition of NordGrid Photonics GmbH by Helios Industrial Holdings for an in-depth Phase 2 investigation. The Authority found no realistic prospect of a substantial lessening of competition in any UK market for industrial laser optics and accordingly cleared the merger unconditionally at Phase 1.
