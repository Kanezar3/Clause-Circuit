IN THE COURT OF CHANCERY OF THE STATE OF DELAWARE
C.A. No. 2026-1192-DEL - Order Denying Preliminary Injunction (16 November 2026)

Upon consideration of Plaintiffs' motion seeking a temporary restraining order and preliminary injunction to halt consummation of the acquisition of NordGrid Photonics GmbH by Helios Industrial Holdings, the Court concludes that Plaintiffs have failed to establish a reasonable probability of success on the merits or irreparable harm. IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that the motion for preliminary injunctive relief is DENIED and DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE. No court injunction, restraining order, or judicial stay currently bars closing of the transaction.
