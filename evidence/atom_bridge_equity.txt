NORDIC SOVEREIGN INFRASTRUCTURE EQUITY FUND
Instrument Reference BRIDGE-EQ-2026-908 - Execution Confirmation (15 November 2026)

The Investment Committee confirms that the definitive EUR 420,000,000 subordinated bridge equity subscription agreement with Helios Industrial Holdings was duly executed and delivered on 15 November 2026. All internal fund approvals have been satisfied, and the committed bridge equity capital is unconditionally available on a certain-funds basis to fund closing of the acquisition of NordGrid Photonics GmbH upon delivery of a standard closing notice.
